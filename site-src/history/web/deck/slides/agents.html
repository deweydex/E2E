2024 onwards

HTML

Some visitors are not people

AI agents can now open a page, read it, click buttons and fill in forms for a person. They read the HTML much as a screen reader does.

A real button, a labelled input and proper headings help a blind visitor, a search engine and an agent at the same time. A div pretending to be a button helps none of them.

Discuss

Should a site be able to say no to AI crawlers? Who decides?

If an AI answer uses your page, what do you think you are owed?

Would you publish a site an AI wrote without reading the code?

The point for students: semantic HTML was always about accessibility, and it now also decides how well machines understand a site. Keep the discussion open. These questions are being argued in courts and parliaments right now, and reasonable people disagree. Give each side a fair hearing.

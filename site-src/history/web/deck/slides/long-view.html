Sources: Pew Research Center, Mobile fact sheet; HTTP Archive, Web Almanac CSS chapter 2022; W3Techs historical trends; Stack Overflow Developer Surveys 2019, 2021, 2022 and 2024; World Bank and ITU. Each line measures a different group.

Smartphones went from a third of US adults to nine in ten in about a decade, and pages had to work on them. Flexbox was on half of pages within two years of being everywhere; Grid, from 2017, was on only 12 per cent by 2022. TypeScript grew among developers every year, while Flash shrank for a decade after its peak. The lines measure different things: some count people, some developers, some websites. Compare their shapes and speeds, not their heights.

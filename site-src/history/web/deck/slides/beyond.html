Beyond Web 2.0

What came next, and what it raises

A changeA question it raises
Phones and appsIs a website or an app the better choice for a given job?
The cloudWho owns the servers our data lives on, and where are they?
A few very large platformsWhat happens when a handful of companies host most of what we read?
Tracking and advertisingHow much does a site learn about its visitors, and who decides?
AI-generated contentHow can a reader tell who, or what, wrote a page?

There is no single agreed name for this period. Some people call it Web 3.0, and they mean different things by it.

This slide is for discussion, not for memorising, so it carries no colour tag. A good answer names one change since Web 2.0 and one concern it raises, in the speaker’s own words. Encourage people to choose a change they care about.

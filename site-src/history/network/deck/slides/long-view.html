Sources: Pew Research Center, Mobile fact sheet; HTTP Archive, Web Almanac CSS chapter 2022; W3Techs historical trends; Stack Overflow Developer Surveys 2019, 2021, 2022 and 2024; World Bank and ITU. Each line measures a different group.

Internet use climbed for thirty years and has still not reached everyone. Smartphones spread faster than the internet itself, on top of networks already built. HTTP/2 rose to half of sites, then gave ground to HTTP/3: a protocol can be replaced before it finishes spreading. The lines measure different things: some count people, some developers, some websites. Compare their shapes and speeds, not their heights.

2019 onwards

Network

5G, fibre and Wi-Fi 6

The last mile got much faster in the late 2010s. Fibre to the home, 5G mobile and Wi-Fi 6 all arrived within a few years of each other.

Headline speeds now reach a gigabit or more. The web did not need that speed for pages; video, games and large downloads used it.

April 2019

The first 5G phone networks open, in South Korea and the United States.

2019

Wi-Fi 6 devices arrive.

2020s

Fibre replaces copper phone lines in many homes.

The pattern from chapter 3 repeats: a faster pipe arrives, and new uses fill it. Ask the class what they do on a phone now that would have been impossible in 2010.

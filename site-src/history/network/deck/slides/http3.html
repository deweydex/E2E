HTTP/3: a new road for mobile

TCP delivers bytes strictly in order, so one lost packet holds up everything behind it. On a patchy mobile signal that happens often.

QUIC, standardised in May 2021, replaces TCP for the web. It runs over UDP, a simpler protocol, and handles each stream on its own, so one loss holds up only its own stream. HTTP/3, in June 2022, is HTTP over QUIC.

2013

Google starts testing QUIC in Chrome.

May 2021

QUIC becomes a standard.

June 2022

HTTP/3 becomes a standard.

QUIC also survives a change of network. A phone that walks out of Wi-Fi onto 4G can keep the same connection, where TCP would have to start again.

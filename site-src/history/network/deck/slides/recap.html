Questions to talk over

Each question has more than one good answer. Pick one, and back it up with an example from the talk.

  1. Which change in this history most changed what a website could do?
  2. What is the difference between bandwidth and latency? Which matters more for a phone?
  3. Why did pages get heavier as networks got faster?
  4. Who pays for a heavy page, and how?
  5. Load a site with the network throttled. What appears first, and what makes you wait?
The last question needs the developer tools: open the Network tab, choose a slow throttling preset, and reload. Pairs can compare two sites they use often.

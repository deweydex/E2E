Broadband changes what a page can do

Always on

DSL over phone lines and cable modems over TV cables gave connections that stayed open, at a megabit or more.

Scripts in the background

With the connection always there, a page could ask the server for small updates every few seconds. That is the Ajax pattern of 2005.

Gmail, 2004 · Google Maps, 2005

Video

YouTube launched in 2005. Streaming video to anyone needed both broadband homes and cheap bandwidth for the server.

Each of these was possible earlier in a lab. Broadband made it possible for ordinary visitors.

Ask the class to imagine Google Maps on a 28.8 modem: every drag of the map fetches new image tiles. The design only works when fetching is cheap and quick.

Sources: Pew Research Center, Mobile fact sheet; HTTP Archive, Web Almanac CSS chapter 2022; W3Techs historical trends; Stack Overflow Developer Surveys 2019, 2021, 2022 and 2024; World Bank and ITU. Each line measures a different group.

PHP fell slowly while Node and TypeScript rose slowly: servers change over decades, not years. HTTPS went from about a quarter of sites to nearly nine in ten in seven years, once certificates were free. In 2024, 51 per cent of developers in the Stack Overflow survey used SQL, as many as used Python. The lines measure different things: some count people, some developers, some websites. Compare their shapes and speeds, not their heights.

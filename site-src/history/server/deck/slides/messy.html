Overlap. PHP is from 1995, and three sites in four still ran it in 2025.

Different speeds. Node arrived in 2009 but reached 4 per cent of sites only in 2025.

Dependency. A change of language means rewriting a working site, so servers change far more slowly than browsers.

Source: W3Techs surveys of the top ten million websites, 1 January each year.

A date on a slide marks when something appeared, not when everyone used it, and on the server the gap is widest. Rewriting a working site is expensive, so old languages keep running for decades. The survey can only see the language on sites that reveal it, which is why the shares are of sites whose language is known.
